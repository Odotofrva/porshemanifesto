Entry 001 / Opening note

WHY THE PANAMERA?

Before the modifications, before the tire lettering, before the final look — there's a reason this particular Porsche became the one.

The 911 gets the spotlight, and it's earned every bit of it. But I always felt a different energy from the Panamera. It's the long, low silhouette. The four-door confidence. The idea that performance and everyday life can exist in the same space.

This journal is where I put the learning on record. Not pretending to be a master mechanic. Not skipping the homework. Just showing what it looks like when an entrepreneur studies a machine and builds a vision one lesson at a time.

New field notes will be posted here weekly as there are genuine updates to share. Expect research on maintenance, diagnostic systems, the first-generation Panamera, and the details behind the MENOCLONE Motorsports design.

— OdotNYC / Originality Has Origins.
